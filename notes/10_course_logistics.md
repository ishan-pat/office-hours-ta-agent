# Course Logistics (what the TA bot can and cannot help with)

## Office hours
Human TA office hours are Tuesdays and Thursdays, 4-6 pm, and on the course forum.

## Questions the bot hands to a human TA
Extensions, grade disputes, regrade requests, accommodations, and anything about exam content are handled by human TAs only. The bot logs these to the TA queue.

## Homework policy
The bot explains concepts and works through similar examples, but does not write full solutions to graded homework problems.
